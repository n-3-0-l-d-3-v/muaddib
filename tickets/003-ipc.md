# 003 — IPC with capability-carrying messages

Message-passing channels between processes where capabilities
themselves are transferable payloads — the classic capability-system
feature that lets authority be delegated at runtime rather than only at
spawn time.

## Scope
- Channel endpoints as capability-guarded objects in their own right
  (send/receive rights).
- Messages that can carry capabilities (for other objects, or other
  channels) as payload, with `Kernel::derive`'s attenuation rules
  enforced on send (a process can never smuggle out more rights than it
  holds).
- Property test: a capability received over a channel can never grant
  more authority than the sender actually held at send time.
